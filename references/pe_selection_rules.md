## Interpretation notes

- These cases are the validated worked examples for PE selection.
- They reinforce the rules that superseded rows, historical rows, and future-dated rows must not create PEs.
- They also show that each selected partition can yield one PE per academic plan entry, including major/minor combinations.
- Completed-program cases should be picked up only when the completion term is within the last 3 terms of the current term.
